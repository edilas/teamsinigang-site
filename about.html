🍲 The Studio

We make games the way we make soup.

The gaming arm of AGOS LLC — Filipino-flavored and always cooking something new.

We named ourselves after sinigang on purpose. It's the dish that hits you with sourness first, then keeps you coming back for one more spoonful. That's exactly the kind of games we want to make: quick to start, hard to put down, and unmistakably ours.

Team Sinigang is the gaming arm of AGOS LLC. This is where we build original games, experiment with interactive ideas, and let the playful side of the company run a little wild.

Our browser games are free to play and run right in your browser — no installs, no accounts, no fuss. Dodge collapsing black holes in Fingularity (now also our first iPhone app, out on the App Store), sprint through the canopy in Jungle Sprint, solve flavor riddles in Puzzle Pot, or hold the line at Mactan in Lapu-Lapu: Defender of Mactan. On Android, our arcade space shooter Starfall: Shattered Continuum is out on Google Play.

We also run Sinigaming — our corner for hints, tips, and guides on the games we're obsessed with, written by players for players. And between rounds, stick around for the food: we share the recipes we actually cook, because the culture behind the name matters to us as much as the games do.

Who makes this

Team Sinigang is run by a working IT guy in Los Angeles who builds these games and writes these pages after hours. It's a family operation: he plays alongside his wife and their two sons, an 8-year-old and an 18-year-old who just headed off to college nearby, gaming machine in tow. With an 8-year-old in the house, most of the gaming here happens after bedtime-story time.

He's been playing Paladins since tabletop D&D and Advanced D&D, through World of Warcraft and plenty of games since, and right now it's Diablo IV. Every guide on Sinigaming comes from games he's actually played, and the scores reflect how much he enjoyed them, not a critic's checklist.
