Balaraw: Blood Island Is Soooo essssScareeeee

Published November 2, 2026 · By Team Sinigang · Spoiler-free

HorrorSeries · 6 episodesFilipinoOur score: 4 / 5

Netflix Philippines went straight for the folklore with this one, and we're here for it. Balaraw: Blood Island follows Sabina Tejeros (Janine Gutierrez), who has never gotten over her mother Rebecca (Agot Isidro) vanishing years ago. When a clue points to Balaraw, an island most local fishermen write off as a legend, she goes anyway, with her girlfriend Bulak (Charlie Dizon) right beside her.

Rae Red writes and directs, reuniting with Gutierrez after Babae at Baril, and you can feel that trust. Gutierrez carries the grief of a kid who never got an answer, and Dizon gives Bulak a steady warmth that makes you worry about her the second they step off the boat.

🩸 Why It Works

This isn't jump-scare-every-five-minutes horror. It's island dread: the kind where the trees feel like they're listening and every villager smiles one beat too long. The scares come from Philippine folklore, so if you grew up with lola telling you not to whistle at night, a lot of this is going to hit a nerve you forgot you had.

Underneath the blood and shadows it's a family story. Sabina isn't only looking for her mom; she's digging into secrets inside her own family, and the island makes her pay for every answer. Six episodes keeps it tight, with no filler.

🌊 Where It Wobbles

If you want nonstop creature action, the slower episodes ask for some patience. The mystery is handed out carefully, and a couple of stretches lean on mood more than momentum. Stick with it. The veteran supporting cast keeps the island feeling lived-in and wrong the whole way through.

🛏️ Field report from our bedroom
Soooo essssScareeeee

Full disclosure: my wife has officially retired from watching this with me. We do the classic setup: lights off, in bed, covers pulled halfway over our faces. The show handles its scares just fine. The problem is me. I gasp, I flinch, I grab her arm at exactly the wrong moment, and apparently that's a second jumpscare she never sees coming. She says the show is scary, but I'm the part that's unpredictable. So I'm finishing it solo. Pray for me.

🍲 Our Take

  • ✅ Real Filipino folk horror, rooted in myths a lot of us grew up hearing.
  • ✅ Two leads you actually care about, so the scares land harder.
  • ✅ Tight at six episodes. A perfect weekend binge.
  • ⚠️ Slow burn in spots. Mood first, monsters second.
  • 🌕 Score: 4 out of 5 blood moons.

One of the best things Netflix Philippines has put out. Watch it at night. Just maybe warn whoever's next to you.
