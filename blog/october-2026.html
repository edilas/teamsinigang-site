📰 What We're Playing · Monthly Roundup

What We're Playing — October 2026

Published October 2026 · By Team Sinigang

A quick confession before we start: gaming around here happens after bedtime. Team Sinigang is run by a working IT guy in Los Angeles with an 8-year-old son, so the controller doesn't come out until homework is done and story time is over. That usually means an hour or two a night. Here's where those hours went this month.

🌌 Big News: Fingularity Is Out on iPhone

Before the games we played, the one we made. Fingularity, our swipe-to-destroy black hole game, is now live on the App Store. It started as a browser game here on Team Sinigang, and this month it became our first native iPhone app, published by AGOS LLC.

🌌 Fingularity for iPhone
Free download · iOS 16 or later · No ads

The free campaign runs ten levels and ends with the Devourer, a five-shell cosmic boss you have to cut apart while keeping the VoyaDur 1 probe alive. Beat it, and a one-time $4.99 unlock opens the Endless Collapse: Level 11 and beyond, celestial and fleet battles, and three rotating bosses (the Archdemon, the Dreadnought, and the Devourer). There's a Game Center leaderboard and haptics on supported phones.

Get Fingularity on the App Store →  ·  Game page & support

Not on an iPhone? The original 10-level campaign is still free to play in your browser. And if you do grab the app, a rating or review on the App Store goes a long way for a small studio like ours.

🚀 And on Android: Starfall: Shattered Continuum

One launch wasn't enough for October. Starfall: Shattered Continuum, our arcade space shooter, is now live on Google Play. It's our first Android game, also published by AGOS LLC, and it's a different flavor from Fingularity: a vertical shooter where you dodge, blast, and upgrade your way through a shattered universe.

🚀 Starfall: Shattered Continuum for Android
Free download · Full game $2.99 · No ads, no loot boxes

The campaign runs 200 levels, broken up by boss fights like the Warden, the Star Leviathan, and a Dreadnought battlecruiser. You collect scrap mid-fight and spend it in the hangar on new ships (Dart, Vulcan, Spectre, Nova, Titan) and permanent upgrades, with shields, nova bombs, and time dilation for when it gets ugly. The first ten levels are free, and a one-time $2.99 purchase unlocks the other 190.

Get Starfall on Google Play →  ·  Game page & support

Same ask as above: if you play it, a rating on Google Play helps a lot.

👿 Diablo IV, and a Lifelong Paladin Habit

I've been playing Paladins for a long time. It started with tabletop D&D and Advanced D&D, carried over into World of Warcraft and a lot of other games since, and now it's Diablo IV. There's something about the holy warrior who wades into the worst of it and comes out swinging that I keep coming back to. So when Blizzard added the Paladin in the Lord of Hatred expansion, that was it for my evenings.

👿 Diablo IV: Lord of Hatred
Who's playing: Me, nightly, after bedtime

I just finished the Lord of Hatred campaign, which Steam says only about 13% of players have done, at around 116 hours total. I'm running a Disciple Paladin: Blessed Hammer to clear crowds, Consecration when things get rough, and Arbiter of Justice saved for the fights that really matter. The goal right now is level 70. I wrote up the build and everything I've learned in our Diablo IV guide.

Now that the story's done, I'm deciding what's next. It's either another campaign or turning the difficulty up and seeing how long my Paladin survives. Probably both, eventually.

💀 New in the Rotation: Mortal Shell II

Diablo has company. We've started Mortal Shell II, a Soulslike where you possess the bodies of fallen warriors, and we're 3.5 hours in with Proxima and Tiel unlocked. Screenshots and early notes are in our first look.

🌴 On the Horizon: GTA 6

Rockstar has Grand Theft Auto VI slated for November 19 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, with PC expected later. I'm thinking about it. But with an 8-year-old in the house, GTA is very much an after-bedtime game. Story time comes first, Vice City second.

🎮 New on Sinigaming: Honest Scores

We re-scored every guide this month. The numbers now reflect how much we actually enjoyed each game, not a critic's checklist.
