How it plays

One gesture. Increasing chaos.

Fingularity is built around the thing a touchscreen does best: immediate physical input. No tutorial maze. See danger, swipe it away, keep going.

●

Spot the singularity

Black holes open across the screen and grow by the second. The longer a level runs, the more of them demand your attention at once.

↗

Swipe it away

Cut across event horizons with fast, deliberate swipes to shrink and destroy them before they swallow everything nearby.

🛰

Protect VoyaDur 1

Keep the probe alive through a level and your points double. Lose it and the universe gets a lot lonelier.

☄

Beat the Devourer

Level 10 ends with a five-shell cosmic boss. Sever its arms, withstand its gravity, and break every shell.

◎

Game Center

Post your best run to the Game Center high-score leaderboard and see how you stack up — without turning the game into a social network.

✦

Made for touch

Haptic feedback on supported iPhones, procedural sound, and atmospheric space visuals — designed around fingers, not tiny virtual buttons.

∞

  • The Dreadnought — destroy its weapons and expose the reactor.
  • The Devourer — break every shell while keeping VoyaDur 1 alive.

One purchase, no subscription, no ads. Family Sharing supported where available.

The Endless Collapse: Level 11 and beyond, with celestial and fleet battlefields and three rotating bosses — the Archdemon, the Dreadnought, and the Devourer. It's a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

Not yet. Fingularity is iPhone-only for now. Android players can play the free web demo on this page in the meantime, and our arcade space shooter Starfall: Shattered Continuum is out now on Google Play.

Yes. The free web demo runs the original 10-level campaign with no download. Endless mode, Game Center, and haptics are only in the iPhone app.

Does it use Game Center?

Yes. The iPhone app has a Game Center high-score leaderboard. Availability depends on your device and Game Center settings.

Does the app have ads or collect data?

No ads, and the App Store privacy label lists no data collected.
