Elden Ring: Nightreign — Complete Guide

FromSoftware · Released May 30, 2025 · Standalone Game · PC, PS5, Xbox

Complete Guide Co-op Roguelite ~45 min per run 🌙 9 / 10

🌙 This is NOT Elden Ring with multiplayer bolted on

Nightreign is a completely different game. It's a roguelite — your gear and stats reset each run. The map is procedurally generated. You play a preset Nightfarer class instead of building your own character. Think Apex Legends meets Elden Ring combat, with a shrinking zone mechanic. Each expedition takes roughly 30–45 minutes.

Nightreign is FromSoftware's most accessible game for players with limited time. Each run is a complete experience — win or lose, you're done in under an hour. There's no massive save file to maintain, no 30-minute dungeon to redo. Pick it up on a weeknight and feel satisfied in one session.

🌍 How an Expedition Works

The 3-day structure

  • 1
    3-day structure: loot → loot → Nightlord Every expedition follows the same structure. Days 1 and 2 are your prep phase: run around the Limveld map (a procedurally generated remix of Limgrave), fight enemies, find better weapons, level up at Sites of Grace, and hunt mini-bosses. Day 3 is the Nightlord — a massive boss you must defeat together. The whole thing takes roughly 30–45 minutes.
  • 2
    The Night's Tide will kill you if you ignore it The Night's Tide is a shrinking ring (like a battle royale zone) that closes in as each day progresses. Standing in it drains HP, drops a character level, and costs Runes. Each day lasts about 15 minutes — keep one eye on the map and always know where you're being pushed. The tide doesn't affect enemies, only you.
  • 3
    Losing a run is not wasted time — Relics carry over Failed expeditions still grant Relics — permanent items equipped at the Roundtable Hold between runs. Relics provide passive buffs that carry into every future expedition. Over time, a strong Relic loadout makes you significantly more powerful before you've even picked up a weapon in Limveld. Every run — win or lose — is progress.

⚡ Surviving Days 1 & 2

How to make the most of your prep time

  • 5
    Always be upgrading your weapon — smithing stones are everywhere Unlike Elden Ring where you carefully choose what to upgrade, in Nightreign you should constantly swap and upgrade weapons. Smithing stones drop from enemies, appear in chests, and are sold by merchants. Use them immediately — there's no carry-over between runs, so hoarding is pointless. Bigger numbers almost always win.
  • 6
    Dual-wield same weapon types for double attacks Equipping two weapons of the same type (two daggers, two longswords) lets you swing both simultaneously with your left-hand attack. This is a significant DPS increase — look for matching weapon types during your loot runs on Days 1 and 2.
  • 7
    Use consumables on the ground without pocketing them You can use items directly off the ground without putting them in inventory. Grease, boluses, flasks — use them on the spot. Items with status-effect cures give a small resistance buff for the rest of the run. In a roguelite, resources you hoard are resources wasted.
  • 8
    Not every enemy needs to die — be selective You're on a 15-minute timer each day. Large groups of weak enemies eat your clock without meaningful rewards. Prioritize: mini-bosses (guaranteed good loot), Sites of Grace (leveling), merchants (smithing stones), and map objectives. Skip packs that offer nothing special.
  • 9
    Mark good weapons for teammates Hold Triangle/Y + L3 to mark an item in the world. It appears on your teammates' maps and screens. A sword useless to a Recluse mage might be a game-changer for a Raider. Passing gear between players is one of the big advantages of co-op Nightreign.
🏆 The Nightlord

Day 3 — the fight you've been preparing for

  • 10
    If all three players go down simultaneously — it's over During the Day 3 Nightlord fight, if all allies are downed at the same moment it's an instant defeat. When things get bad, one player should prioritize surviving and reviving teammates — not everyone charging in at once. One person alive keeps the run alive.
  • 11
    Coordinate Ultimate Arts — don't use all three at once Each Nightfarer's Ultimate Art is a powerful recharging ability. Stagger them through the fight rather than burning all three in the first phase. One player opens with their Ultimate, the other two hold theirs for the next phase. Consistent burst across the whole fight beats front-loading damage.
  • 12
    Buy FP-restoring items before Day 3 for magic users Any Nightfarer using spells or Character Skills burns through FP fast. Stock FP-restoring items from merchants before the Nightlord fight begins. Running out of FP mid-Nightlord effectively removes one player's toolkit from the fight.
🔮 The Relic System

Your permanent progression — the soul of long-term Nightreign

  • 13
    Relics are the only thing that carries across runs — take them seriously You can equip up to 6 Relics at once from the Roundtable Hold. They provide passive buffs to attributes, damage types, and conditional bonuses. Unlock more Relic slots by buying character Goblets from the Small Jar Bazaar using Murks (earned from runs). Every run — win or lose — is a Relic-farming session.
  • 14
    Coordinate your squad's Relics — some scale with multiple users Certain Relics scale exponentially when multiple players run the same one. A squad heal Relic that triggers when one player uses a flask becomes a full-team AoE heal if all three carry it. Before entering an expedition together, coordinate Relic setups and assign roles: who tanks, who bursts, who supports.
  • 15
    Complete Remembrance questlines for unique Relics In the Roundtable Hold Journal, each Nightfarer has a Remembrance — an optional story quest triggered by finding Memory Fragments during runs (marked with an orange flag on the map). Completing them gives big Rune payouts and unlocks special Relics that significantly power up that character's key ability.
🌙 The Sinigaming Verdict

Our take on Nightreign

  • ✅ The best FromSoftware game for players with limited session time — each run is a complete experience
  • ✅ Co-op genuinely improves it — squad synergy adds real depth beyond just having help
  • ✅ Even failed runs progress your Relic loadout — no session feels wasted
  • ⚠️ This is NOT a continuation of Elden Ring's story — it's a side-universe spinoff
  • ⚠️ Procedural randomness means some runs are loot-rich and others feel dry — that's the roguelite deal
  • 💡 Start with Wylder — learn the systems — then experiment freely with other Nightfarers
  • 💡 ~30–45 min per expedition · Perfect for a weeknight session after the kids are in bed
