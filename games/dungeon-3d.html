What is Dungeon of the Ancients 3D?

Dungeon of the Ancients 3D is a first-person roguelike dungeon crawl — inspired by the classic 1980s dungeon crawlers that defined the genre. Roll your champion, choose your blade, and descend into cursed halls filled with monsters, traps, and treasure.

Each floor goes deeper and gets harder. Slay monsters, claim chests, survive traps, and face the boss before descending further. How deep can you go?

🎮 How to Play

Controls & Gameplay

WASD

Move & Turn

WASD or Arrow Keys to move forward, back, and turn left/right through the dungeon.

Q / E

Strafe

Sidestep left or right without turning — essential for dodging enemy attacks.

F / Space

Shoot Bow

Fire your bow at enemies. Ammo is limited — pick your shots.

P

Drink Potion

Use a healing potion when you're in trouble. Find more as you descend.

📊 Scoring

How points work

Chests & Traps

10× depth multiplier

Monster Kills

20× depth multiplier

Floor Descent

50× depth multiplier

Boss Slain

100× depth multiplier

💡 Tips

How to score higher

Walk into monsters to engage in melee — don't waste arrows on enemies you can reach. Save potions for boss fights rather than using them on regular floors. Every floor you descend multiplies your score — surviving to deeper floors is worth far more than killing everything on the way down.
