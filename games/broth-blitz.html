What is Broth Blitz?

Broth Blitz is a fast-paced arcade game built around real Filipino soup recipes. Cook Sinigang, Bulalo, Tinola, and more — each dish has a specific sequence of ingredients, and you need to tap the right one before the timer expires.

Three lives, escalating levels, and a global leaderboard. How many dishes can you nail before the heat gets to you?

🎮 How to Play

Controls & Gameplay

📋

Follow the Recipe

Each level gives you a Filipino soup recipe to cook — add ingredients in the correct order.

⏱️

Beat the Timer

Every ingredient has a time limit. Tap the right one before the clock runs out.

✅

Right = Points

Correct ingredient in time = 100 pts + time bonus. Wrong or slow = lose a life.

🏆

Leaderboard

Your best score goes on the shared leaderboard. Compete with everyone.

💡 Tips

How to score higher

Read the recipe order before the level starts — each dish follows a real cooking sequence, which makes the order logical once you know it. Sour agents go in early for most soups; protein goes in before vegetables. The more Filipino food you've eaten, the more the order will feel natural.
