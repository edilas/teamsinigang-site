How it plays

Classic arcade shooter. Modern fleet.

Starfall is a vertical shooter in the old-school arcade tradition: your ship at the bottom, the whole hostile sky above. What's new is everything you carry between runs.

✦

Survive the waves

Weave through enemy fire and drifting space debris. Every level adds pressure, and a clean run is worth bragging about.

⬡

Collect scrap

Scrap is the currency of a shattered universe. Pick it up mid-fight and spend it in the hangar on new ships and permanent upgrades.

✹

Pull out the big tools

Shields absorb the hit you didn't see coming, nova bombs clear the screen, and time dilation slows the chaos when you need a breath.

☄

Break the bosses

Massive bosses guard the Continuum, each with its own attack patterns. Watch the health bar, learn the pattern, punish the opening.

▲

Build your fleet

Unlock and upgrade different ships, each with its own balance of guns, speed, and hull, then customize how your ship and weapons look.

∞

200 levels deep

The campaign runs 200 levels, and high scores get harder to push as the challenge climbs. There's a leaderboard for when you do.
