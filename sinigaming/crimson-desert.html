Crimson Desert — Guide for Skyrim Veterans

Pearl Abyss · Released March 19, 2026 · PC · Open World Action RPG

Complete GuideHard Combat Open World~50–80 hrs 🗡️ 8.5 / 10
Skyrim vs Crimson DesertFirst Hour ProgressionExplorationVerdict

Crimson Desert is Pearl Abyss's massive open-world action RPG set in the land of Pywel — roughly twice the size of Skyrim and larger than Red Dead Redemption 2's map. It's stunning to explore, brutal in combat, and built very differently from anything Bethesda ever made. This guide is written for Skyrim veterans who have 100% completed Tamriel and want to know exactly what to expect — and more importantly, what assumptions to drop at the door.

🗺️ Skyrim vs Crimson Desert

What's the same, what's completely different

Feature⚔️ Skyrim🗡️ Crimson Desert

🏙️ First Hour

Don't leave Hernand — your starting city

  • 1
    Spend real time in Hernand — this is your WhiterunResist the urge to sprint into the wild. Talk to every named NPC, find the Butcher, Grocer, and Smithy, and grab every favor quest. These tasks ease you into combat systems and reward you with gear and reputation before the world demands more of you.
  • 2
    Get through Chapter 4 before heavy free-roamingLarge portions of the map start nearly empty. They unlock and come alive as you advance the main story through Chapter 4. Push the story first — the world rewards you massively when it opens up.
  • 3
    Use a controller — seriouslyThe combat system was built with a controller in mind. Multiple reviewers describe mouse and keyboard as "very, very difficult" in combat. Grab your PS or Xbox controller from hour one.
⚡ Progression — Abyss Artifacts

There are no levels here — here's what replaces them

  • 4
    Abyss Artifacts = Skyrim skill points + perk points combinedThere are no XP bars. You find glowing Abyss Artifacts throughout the world. They let you revive at 30% HP on death and invest points into stats and skills. Collect them religiously — they are the entire progression system.
  • 5
    Question marks on the map hide fast travel points AND ArtifactsRoughly 40% of map question marks are Abyss Nexus fast travel points. The rest are Abyss Cressets that reward Artifacts. Clear every single one — you unlock fast travel AND get stronger simultaneously. Think of this like your Word Wall hunt.
  • 6
    Invest in Stamina early — it runs everythingStamina governs your ability to block, attack, sprint, and dodge. Early Abyss Artifact points into Stamina will feel immediately impactful in every combat encounter.
🌍 Exploration & Combat

How to thrive in Pywel

  • 7
    Ring bells on tall structures to reveal the mapRinging bells on elevated structures unlocks the regional map with points of interest — Pywel's version of climbing a tower. Always go high. Always ring the bell. This is how you find Artifact locations, quests, and merchants before stumbling into them.
  • 8
    Bosses will destroy you early — stockpile foodBoss encounters are notoriously demanding early in the game. Hunt animals for meat, buy from the Butcher and Grocer, and cook at Campfires. Bring as much food as you can into every boss fight. Food is your primary healing system.
  • 9
    Combat is about timing — not stat-checkingCrimson Desert rewards reading enemy behavior and mastering the dodge, block, and counter system. A weapon that fits your rhythm will outperform a higher-stat weapon you haven't learned. Read attacks before swinging.
  • 10
    Do commissions constantly for Silver and Contribution RankFaction quests and commissions raise your Contribution Rank, which unlocks unique regional gear. This is your best income loop and the key to staying competitive on equipment throughout the game.
🍲 The Sinigaming Verdict

Our take on Crimson Desert

🕹️ Our SiniPlay take

Great, too. But Diablo kept calling us back.

  • ✅ Open-world exploration feels like a spiritual successor to Skyrim — and surpasses it in scale
  • ✅ Completionist instincts are perfectly suited here — enormous amount to find
  • ⚠️ Combat, progression, and fast travel are completely different — don't expect to adapt in the first few hours
  • ⚠️ Cinematic, focused story — you're playing Kliff's story, not writing your own
  • 💡 Get through Chapter 4 before going full explorer mode — the world opens up massively after
  • 💡 Estimated 50–80+ hours to see everything
