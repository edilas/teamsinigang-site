Elden Ring: Nightreign — Complete Guide

FromSoftware · Released May 30, 2025 · Standalone Game · PC, PS5, Xbox

Complete GuideCo-op Roguelite ~45 min per run🌙 8.5 / 10
How It WorksNightfarer Classes Days 1 & 2The Nightlord Relic SystemVerdict

🌙 This is NOT Elden Ring with multiplayer bolted on

Nightreign is a completely different game. It's a roguelite — your gear and stats reset each run. The map is procedurally generated. You play a preset Nightfarer class. Think Apex Legends meets Elden Ring combat, with a shrinking zone mechanic. Each expedition takes roughly 30–45 minutes.

Nightreign is FromSoftware's most accessible game for players with limited time. Each run is a complete experience — win or lose, you're done in under an hour.

🌍 How an Expedition Works

The 3-day structure

  • 1
    3-day structure: loot → loot → NightlordDays 1 and 2 are prep: run around the Limveld map, fight enemies, find better weapons, level up at Sites of Grace, hunt mini-bosses. Day 3 is the Nightlord — a massive boss you must defeat together. The whole run takes 30–45 minutes.
  • 2
    The Night's Tide will kill you if you ignore itThe Night's Tide is a shrinking ring (like a battle royale zone) that closes in as each day progresses. Standing in it drains HP, drops a character level, and costs Runes. Keep one eye on the map and always know where you're being pushed.
  • 3
    Losing a run is not wasted time — Relics carry overFailed expeditions still grant Relics — permanent items that carry into every future expedition. Over time, a strong Relic loadout makes you significantly more powerful before you've even picked up a weapon. Every run — win or lose — is progress.

Guardian is the second-best learning class — high defense lets you make mistakes without dying instantly. Learn the map on these two before trying Executor or Revenant.
⚡ Surviving Days 1 & 2

How to make the most of your prep time

  • 5
    Always be upgrading your weapon — smithing stones are everywhereIn Nightreign constantly swap and upgrade weapons. Smithing stones drop everywhere. Use them immediately — there's no carry-over between runs, so hoarding is pointless.
  • 6
    Dual-wield same weapon types for double attacksEquipping two weapons of the same type lets you swing both simultaneously with your left-hand attack. A significant DPS increase — look for matching weapon types during your loot runs.
  • 7
    Use consumables on the ground without pocketing themYou can use items directly off the ground without putting them in inventory. In a roguelite, resources you hoard are resources wasted. Use them on the spot.
  • 8
    Not every enemy needs to die — be selectiveYou're on a 15-minute timer each day. Prioritize: mini-bosses (guaranteed good loot), Sites of Grace (leveling), merchants (smithing stones), and map objectives. Skip packs that offer nothing special.
  • 9
    Mark good weapons for teammatesHold Triangle/Y + L3 to mark an item in the world. A sword useless to a Recluse might be a game-changer for a Raider. Passing gear between players is one of the big co-op advantages of Nightreign.
🏆 The Nightlord

Day 3 — the fight you've been preparing for

  • 10
    If all three players go down simultaneously — it's overDuring the Nightlord fight, if all allies are downed at the same moment it's an instant defeat. When things get bad, one player should prioritize surviving and reviving teammates rather than everyone charging in at once.
  • 11
    Coordinate Ultimate Arts — don't use all three at onceStagger them through the fight rather than burning all three in the first phase. Consistent burst across the whole fight beats front-loading damage.
  • 12
    Buy FP-restoring items before Day 3 for magic usersAny Nightfarer using spells burns through FP fast. Stock FP-restoring items from merchants before the Nightlord fight begins. Running out of FP effectively removes one player's toolkit from the fight.
🔮 The Relic System

Your permanent progression

  • 13
    Relics are the only thing that carries across runs — take them seriouslyYou can equip up to 6 Relics at once from the Roundtable Hold. They provide passive buffs to attributes, damage types, and conditional bonuses. Unlock more slots by buying character Goblets from the Small Jar Bazaar using Murks (earned from runs).
  • 14
    Coordinate your squad's Relics — some scale with multiple usersCertain Relics scale exponentially when multiple players run the same one. Before entering an expedition together, coordinate Relic setups — who tanks, who bursts, who supports.
  • 15
    Complete Remembrance questlines for unique RelicsEach Nightfarer has a Remembrance in the Roundtable Hold Journal — an optional story quest triggered by finding Memory Fragments during runs (orange flag on the map). Completing them gives big Rune payouts and unlocks special Relics.
🌙 The Sinigaming Verdict

Our take on Nightreign

🕹️ Our SiniPlay take

Very good, but we never seem to have the time for full campaigns.

  • ✅ The best FromSoftware game for players with limited session time
  • ✅ Co-op genuinely improves it — squad synergy adds real depth
  • ✅ Even failed runs progress your Relic loadout — no session feels wasted
  • ⚠️ This is NOT a continuation of Elden Ring's story — it's a side-universe spinoff
  • ⚠️ Procedural randomness means some runs are loot-rich and others feel dry
  • 💡 Start with Wylder — learn the systems — then experiment freely
  • 💡 ~30–45 min per expedition · Perfect for a weeknight session
