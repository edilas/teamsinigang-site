⚔️ Our Disciple Paladin Build

The skills we actually use

The Paladin picks one of four Oaths (Zealot, Juggernaut, Judicator, or Disciple), which shapes the whole build. Disciple is the angelic one: every time you cast a Disciple skill that has a cooldown, you briefly take Arbiter form, sprouting wings that strike enemies around you while your Disciple skills hit harder. Keep cooldowns rolling and you can stay in Arbiter form for most of a fight. Your resource is Faith, which your Basic skills generate and your bigger skills spend.

  • 4
    Blessed Hammer is our crowd-clearerOur favorite AoE. Blessed Hammer throws hammers that spiral out through packs, and it's cheap on Faith, so you can keep it going. Take the Disciple’s Halo upgrade and it becomes a Disciple skill, with hammers orbiting you so you can walk straight into a crowd.
  • 5
    Holy Bolt for the hammer throwHoly Bolt is a Basic skill that throws a holy hammer and generates Faith to fuel everything else. The Divine Bolt upgrade turns it into a Disciple skill that pierces through enemies and makes them Vulnerable (they take extra damage).
  • 6
    Consecration keeps us aliveConsecration bathes you in Light for several seconds, healing you every second while damaging enemies standing in it. Drop it when a fight gets messy and stand in it. It's not a Disciple skill, but solo it's the best safety net we've found.
  • 7
    Save Arbiter of Justice for the hard fightsThe Paladin's Ultimate. You launch into the sky and crash down, staying in Arbiter form for a long stretch. It has a long cooldown, so we don't waste it on trash packs; we hold it for bosses, elites, and anything that's going sideways.

💡 Why Disciple feels so good

Arbiter form's Wing Strikes hit around you automatically, so the more time you spend in it, the more free damage you're doing. Skills with cooldowns that are Disciple skills are what trigger it, so the build is really about keeping those rolling.

🔱 Lord of Hatred

What the expansion adds

  • 8
    You need the expansion to play PaladinLord of Hatred launched April 28, 2026, adding the Paladin and Warlock and taking the roster to eight classes. Pre-orders got the Paladin early.
  • 9
    The level cap is now 70Up from 60. Blizzard reworked the curve so reaching max level takes about as long as before: the early levels are a bit slower and the back half moves faster. That's our current goal.
  • 10
    Every class got a new skill treeThe skill tree rework is free for everyone, not just expansion owners. If you're coming back from an older season, expect your old build to look different.
  • 11
    The campaign is worth finishingThe story takes you to Skovos to face Mephisto, the Lord of Hatred himself. Steam says only about 13% of players have finished it. If you like the NPCs, this is the best story content Diablo IV has.
⚗️ General Tips

Things that apply to any Paladin run

  • 12
    Don't lock in too earlyYou can respec skills for a gold cost, so experiment. Try a different Oath for a few levels if you're curious; it's cheap to switch back.
  • 13
    Do the side questsThis is where the NPCs shine: smaller, grimmer human stories you'll miss if you only follow the main quest. They also feed your Renown.
  • 14
    Raise Renown in each regionSide quests, dungeons, and Waypoints fill each region's Renown meter, which pays out permanent rewards for all your characters.
  • 15
    Clear dungeons for AspectsFinishing a dungeon for the first time can unlock a Legendary Aspect in your Codex of Power, which you can then imprint onto gear. That's how you get Legendary effects onto items you like.
  • 16
    It's always onlineDiablo IV needs an internet connection even when you play solo, so plan ahead if you're playing away from home.
