ℹ️ About the Game

What is Lapu-Lapu: Defender of Mactan?

Lapu-Lapu: Defender of Mactan is Team Sinigang's most ambitious browser game — a real-time strategy-action game set during the Battle of Mactan on April 27, 1521. Lead Datu Lapu-Lapu's Visayan warriors against Ferdinand Magellan's Spanish forces. Use the terrain, the shallows, and the fighting skill of your people to repel the invasion. This is a real moment in history — the first defeat of European colonizers in what would become the Philippines. Honor it.

🎮 How to Play

Controls & Gameplay

⚔️

Command Your Warriors

Click to direct groups of warriors to intercept Spanish soldiers. Position matters — use the shallows.

🏹

Ranged Attacks

Your archers and javelin throwers work best from elevated ground. Keep them on the ridge and out of melee.

🛡️

Hold the Beach

Spanish forces lose their firearm advantage in shallow water. Draw them in before engaging in force.

👑

The Boss Fight

Magellan appears in the final wave. His armor is strong but has gaps — read his attack patterns and exploit them.

💡 Tips for High Scores

How to do better

Don't engage in open water — the Spanish firearms are devastating at range. Draw them into the shallows where the terrain neutralizes their equipment advantage. Keep your archers elevated throughout — they're your safest and most consistent damage source. For the Magellan fight: his legs are unarmored. Direct your strongest warriors at his lower body and alternate attacks to interrupt his rhythm. Read our full dev log on how we built this game.
