VOID WYRM COLLISION PASS
- Visible Wyrm head and segmented body now have contact collision during normal movement.
- Head-first charge collision follows the moving head plus the visible trailing segments.
- Hit shapes are intentionally inset from the artwork for fair phone-sized near-misses.
- Player shots can now damage visible Wyrm body segments instead of passing through them.
SOLAR SERAPH COLLISION PASS
- Solar Seraph collision now follows the furnace core and all six visible mechanical wings.
- Player contact with solid wing armor now damages consistently during normal movement and charge passes.
- Player shots can hit the visible wing armor instead of passing through it.
- Decorative radiant light rays remain non-solid, and wing collision is slightly inset for fair iPhone near-misses.

EVENT HORIZON JUMP PASS
- After the Level 50 anomaly reveal, pressing Enter the Event Horizon now plays a dedicated animated transition.
- The currently selected ship is shown flying into the edge of the collapsed Singularity before docking at the Level 51 checkpoint.
- The transition can be skipped, and the existing Level 51 hangar checkpoint flow remains intact.
REPEAT ENDINGS + FLAGSHIP CLAIM PASS
- Defeating level 50 now always triggers the campaign-complete / anomaly / Event Horizon sequence, even when you reach it through level select or on repeat clears.
- Defeating level 100 now always triggers the true ending rather than only the first time.
- Entering Infinite Mode after the true ending now plays a new Dreadnought claim transition, showing the captured flagship joining the fleet.
- The Dreadnought remains the post-game reward ship and is intended to be used in Infinite Mode / level 101+ continuation.
POSTGAME SPECIALNESS PASS
- After the Dreadnought claim animation, Starfall now shows a dedicated Infinite Mode intro screen for Level 101+.
- The Battle Cruiser now receives a more explicit flagship-unlocked badge in the hangar until it is purchased.
- Infinite Mode now has a modest postgame escalation: extra waves, elite Black Pursuer appearances, slightly higher enemy cap, and a small health ramp so level 101+ feels distinct.
SIDE-LANE PROJECTILE PRESSURE PASS
- The far-left and far-right edges are no longer reliable projectile safe zones.
- If the player camps near an outer edge for roughly 0.6 seconds, a current boss or ranged enemy sends a small aimed tracking volley into that lane.
- Leaving the edge immediately stops the added pressure; major boss telegraphs/beam attacks are not stacked with the edge volley.
- Tank spreads now rotate toward an edge-hugging player instead of continuing to fire only straight down.
- Core boss patterns and center-lane difficulty remain unchanged.
