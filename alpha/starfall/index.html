Flight controlsDrag anywhere to steer. Your ship fires automatically. Avoid enemy ships, debris, and hostile fire.
Scrap is your scoreEvery hit, destroyed target, pickup, and cleared level adds scrap. Ship purchases and upgrades spend that same score. Once the fleet is maxed, every reward pushes your score higher.
Ship upgradesOwned ships can be permanently upgraded three times. Defeat level 50 to unlock Super Mode and three additional upgrade levels.
Infinite tierDefeat the level-100 Battle Cruiser to unlock upgrade levels 7–10 and make the capital ship available for purchase.
Repair + ShieldEvery level begins with full hull and one shield. Repair restores one hull point. Every destroyed asteroid awards scrap and reactivates your shield.
Persistent dropsShield, Rapid Fire, and Overcharge stack to ×2 with no timer. Weapon boosts remain until your ship is destroyed; shield charges are lost when hit.
Time Dilation BombCreates one 15-second battlefield-wide time field. Current and newly arriving threats are slowed; another pickup refreshes rather than stacks it.
Nova bombTap the lower-right Nova control to damage everything and erase hostile shots.
Nova cometShoot the glowing comet to destroy every active enemy and clear hostile projectiles.
