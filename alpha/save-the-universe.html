ℹ️ About the Game

What is Fingularity?

Fingularity is a fast-paced browser game that runs on any device. Swipe across black holes to destroy them before they expand too far and devour VoyaDur 1. Survive 10 levels of 30 seconds each — each level spawns more black holes than the last. Survive the round and your score doubles.

🎮 How to Play

Controls & Gameplay

🖱️

Swipe to Destroy

Drag across a black hole to shrink and eliminate it before it expands.

⏱️

30 Seconds Per Level

Each of the 10 levels runs for exactly 30 seconds. Survive and your score doubles.

🌍

Protect VoyaDur 1

The planet VoyaDur 1 must survive each round. If it's consumed, the level ends.

🌀

Escalating Spawns

Each level spawns black holes more frequently. By Level 10 it's full chaos.

💡 Tips for High Scores

How to do better

Prioritize holes near VoyaDur 1. Black holes closest to the planet are the most dangerous. Long swipes clear faster than short ones — a clean diagonal swipe across the center of a hole is the most efficient technique. On mobile, use landscape mode for more screen space. Surviving all 10 levels compounds your score multiplier — the late game is where the real points live.
