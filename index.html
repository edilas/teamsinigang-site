The culture behind the name

Why Team Sinigang?

Sinigang is a Filipino sour soup built around contrast: bright acidity, deep savory broth, vegetables, meat or seafood, and the kind of flavor that announces itself immediately. That's the idea behind the studio too—games and projects that are easy to recognize and hard to confuse with everybody else's.
