Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Team Sinigang ("we," "us," or "our") collects when you visit teamsinigang.com (the "Site"), how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the Site, you agree to the practices described here.

Who we are

Team Sinigang is the gaming brand of AGOS LLC. We make free browser games and publish gaming and Filipino-culture content. You can reach us anytime at admin@teamsinigang.com.

Information we collect

Information you give us

If you email us or use our contact form, we receive whatever you choose to send — typically your name, email address, and message. We use it only to reply to you.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our servers and tools may automatically record standard technical data when you visit, such as your browser type, device, general location (country/region), pages viewed, and the date and time of your visit. This helps us understand how the Site is used and keep it working.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We and our partners use them to remember preferences, measure traffic, and serve advertising. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some parts of the Site may not work as well.

Advertising

We display ads through Google AdSense to keep our games free. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info/choices.

For more detail on how Google uses data, see Google's partner sites policy.

Children's privacy

Our games are family-friendly. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. We do not ask visitors to create accounts or submit personal details to play. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us at admin@teamsinigang.com and we will delete it.

How we use information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Site and our games.
  • To respond to messages you send us.
  • To display advertising that helps keep the Site free.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues or abuse.

We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. To make a request, email us at admin@teamsinigang.com.

Data retention & security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes described here, then delete it. We take reasonable measures to protect data, but no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we'll revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email admin@teamsinigang.com.
